# Resolved fluid flow and coupled capsules

`Simulation._configure_culture` couples steady Stokes flow, rigid growing capsules, geometric extracellular transport, and intracellular amounts. The mode has explicit CPU, Metal, and CUDA implementations, conservative division, transactional stepping, and persistent restart state. The [modeling guide](../models/fluid-culture.md) describes configuration, units, examples, and the scientific validation boundary. Existing simulation modes retain their current semantics.

## Physical model and interfaces

`StokesFlowSolver` selects CPU, Metal, or CUDA explicitly. `solve` computes steady incompressible Newtonian Stokes flow on a uniform three-dimensional MAC grid with arbitrary pressure and total-flow patches on its six outer boundaries. Unspecified boundary faces and obstacle voxels are stationary no-slip walls. `FluidGridSpec` has no chemical species count. Its origin denotes the center of voxel zero, matching `SignalGridSpec`; the lower physical boundary is half a spacing below the origin. Obstacle sites and port sites use x-major, z-minor flattening.

Coordinates and returned face velocities use model lengths and times. `length_unit_m` and `time_unit_s` convert these to SI. Viscosity is in Pa s, density in kg/m³, reservoir pressures in Pa, and prescribed port flow in m³/s. Port flow is positive outward. Pressure ports impose normal traction, including the normal viscous stress; tangential traction is zero at the opening. A rate port has a single unknown reservoir pressure and a constraint on total signed volumetric flux. It does not prescribe a pointwise plug profile. Pressure and rate patches may coexist. Empty port site lists select all fluid faces on the declared boundary. Names must be unique, patches cannot overlap, and each patch must belong to a single face-connected fluid component.

Every component without a pressure port receives its own pressure gauge. Its prescribed net outward rate must match its volume source. A sealed chamber at rest is supported; prescribed net inflow or growth in a sealed incompressible chamber is incompatible. Absolute pressure offsets are subtracted during assembly and restored in the returned pressure field. They do not change velocity. There is no velocity rescaling after the solve.

`solve_bodies` adds capsule surface constraints and force/torque balance to the same linear system. `CapsuleBody` stores a persistent unit quaternion in `(w,x,y,z)` order, with its local capsule axis along x. Length excludes the hemispherical caps. Position, radius, length, elongation rate, translation, and angular velocity use model units. Applied forces and torques use N and N m. Fixed bodies have zero rigid translation and rotation, while their prescribed elongation may remain nonzero. Results report body velocities, hydrodynamic loads, marker counts, and measured no-slip residuals.

The constrained system uses the smooth six-point immersed-boundary delta of [Bao et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.07529), with surface markers approximately two fluid spacings apart. Force spreading is the exact transpose of velocity interpolation. The discrete kernel is checked against its moment, nonnegativity, and constant sum-of-squares identities. Capsule radii require at least two fluid spacings; this is a minimum representation check, not an accuracy guarantee. Wall extension is bounded and does not resolve lubrication singularities. Bodies whose kernel reaches an open port are rejected. Independent spatial, marker, wall-gap, and body-drag refinement is required before interpreting quantitative cell hydrodynamics.

Elongation prescribes the corresponding surface velocity and distributes the exact capsule volume rate `pi*r²*length_rate` over interior pressure cells. This interior source exists only in the fictitious fluid used by the immersed-boundary formulation. It represents displaced extracellular medium, not solute production. The solver reports physical divergence and the separate continuity residual after subtracting the source. Capsule geometry is validated against the domain and voxel walls before assembly.

`propose_body_step` returns a candidate bounded substep and its `accepted_dt`; it does not advance an external clock. A frictionless active-set solve adds compressive contact forces to the fluid/body force balance. Contacts cover capsule pairs, domain walls, and obstacle voxels. The minimum gap is specified in metres, with an additional numerical guard of `1e-5` fluid spacings. Candidate translation, rotation, and elongation move any surface point by at most one quarter of a spacing. Quaternion updates use the exponential rotation, and final geometry is checked again. Excess motion or a failed final gap check halves the proposed interval, up to the configured limit. Infeasible contact constraints or failed linear solves reject the candidate. The returned flow belongs to the initial geometry of that substep; transport must use the same initial and candidate geometries before the caller commits either bodies or time.

## Numerical execution

The operator discretizes the symmetric viscous energy `2 mu e:e`, pressure continuity, reservoir constraints, marker no-slip constraints, and rigid-body force balance. One nondimensionalization is used for all backends. Host setup constructs the sparse operator and preconditioner coefficients. CPU Krylov arithmetic uses binary64. Metal and CUDA use native binary32 sparse matvecs, vector updates, and reductions, with Krylov vectors resident on the selected device. Reduction partials and scalar Arnoldi control are accumulated in host binary64. The final candidate is checked against the assembled operator before it is returned.

Unoccupied flow uses a Galerkin velocity multigrid hierarchy with damped Jacobi smoothing and a small coarse inverse. Coupled-body flow uses a threshold incomplete factorization of the entire constrained matrix. Its triangular application currently runs serially on the selected device. This is a performance limit requiring scaling measurements and a parallel triangular solve before large-colony throughput claims. Preconditioner dropping and pivot safeguards do not modify the physical operator or the residual gate.

FGMRES uses a restart length of 120, twice-modified Gram-Schmidt, and explicit true-residual checks. Default normalized tolerances are relative `1e-5` and absolute `1e-7`. Invalid units, incompatible port data, resource estimates above the configured memory limit, singular unconstrained motion, nonfinite results, and nonconvergence are errors. No backend fallback or automatic tolerance relaxation occurs. Calls return candidates and do not mutate caller-owned geometry or simulation state.

## Extracellular transport and biochemical state

`FluidGeometry` clips volume-preserving convex capsule approximations against voxels and retains separate connected extracellular fragments. Open face areas and membrane patches come from the same geometry. Surface approximation bounds reject unresolved gaps. Positive-volume slivers remain in the mesh. Old/new fragment intersections define temporal control volumes without connecting unrelated fluid pockets.

`SoluteTransportSolver` corrects face fluxes to satisfy local geometric volume changes and prescribed total port rates. A residual-refinement loop checks local geometric conservation as well as the global Krylov norm. Backward-Euler upwind advection, diffusion, and membrane uptake advance authoritative amounts. Secretion and reservoir transfers use the same signed fluxes in both sides of their ledgers. Nonfinite or negative results reject the candidate; no clipping or amount redistribution hides a failed solve.

Each cell stores biochemical volume and binary64 species amounts separately from its analytic geometric volume. Growth consumes configured species amounts per biochemical increment and cannot spend material beyond the current intracellular budget. Native intracellular rate plans receive explicit old/new biochemical volumes on all backends. Surface exchange credits the corresponding intracellular species, making imported material available for the following substep. This operator splitting and the transport scheme are first order in time.

Division splits analytic capsule volume, biochemical volume, and intracellular amounts. Daughter cylinders account for the two additional hemispherical caps. The daughter placement and extracellular remap are validated before commit. The remap has no physical duration, hydraulic transient, or reservoir transfer. Instantaneous removal, external pose edits, and cell passage through an outlet are unsupported.

## State ownership and transactions

The simulation owns the persistent poses, biochemical state, extracellular amounts, reservoir totals, and reconstructed geometry. Public state and fragment accessors return copies. `Simulation.step` stages an entire requested interval and commits only after all adaptive substeps pass. A culture-enabled `NativeController` additionally restores regulation state, its RNG, lifecycle changes, and counters on failure. External side effects inside callbacks cannot be restored.

Checkpoint version 11 records the complete configuration and authoritative amounts; geometry is reconstructed and verified on restore. Scene version 5 includes units, fragment volumes and amounts, biochemical state, and reservoir ledgers. Its voxel concentration field is a fluid-volume-weighted presentation mean. Analysis version 5 preserves quantitative binary64 state in separate culture, fluid-fragment, and chemical-transfer tables. Previous supported formats continue to load.

## Validation boundary

Conformance covers device hydraulics and square-duct refinement, immersed-boundary kernel identities, force/torque and no-slip constraints, contact, growth displacement, geometric volume and face closure, diffusion eigenmodes, moving-volume uniform concentration, exchange budgets, restart, and rollback. Tests enumerate the requested native devices and a GPU runtime gate must accompany hardware evidence. CUDA compilation alone is not runtime validation.

Dense contact networks, quantitative drag and near-wall refinement, and large-colony memory/throughput studies remain validation work. The minimum grid resolution and linear-solver tolerance do not establish physical accuracy. Geometry and operator/factor setup run on the host; native GPU execution refers to the numerical linear-algebra operations described above.

## Running the current interfaces

`examples/growing_media.py --backend cpu` advances a growing nutrient-consuming cell in a perfusion-driven device and can export a checkpoint and scene. `examples/physical_flow.py` exercises the standalone hydraulic solver. Select `metal` or `cuda` explicitly for those backends. The fluid, transport, body, geometry, and culture CTest targets cover hydraulics, bodies, geometry, transport, and integrated simulation. Python integration tests cover the public API, persistence, controller transactions, division, scene interchange, and analysis output.
